# Licences of the golden-set images

**The 30 photographs in `images/` are not covered by this repository's MIT
licence.** They are third-party works, redistributed here under their own
terms. `manifest.csv` names the author and licence of every one of them, and
the `source` column links to the original.

Most are openly-licensed photographs from Wikimedia Commons; four are
screenshots captured for this project and released under CC0.

## What this means if you reuse them

Follow the licence on that image's row in `manifest.csv`, not this
repository's. In practice, for the Creative Commons licences involved:

- **CC BY** — credit the author named in `manifest.csv` and link the licence.
- **CC BY-SA** — the same, and derivatives of *that image* must be shared
  under the same terms.
- **CC0 / public domain** — no obligation.

## Share-alike does not reach the source code

A CC BY-SA image's share-alike obligation covers that image and works derived
from it. It does **not** extend to the Python, Kotlin or configuration in this
project. The code stays MIT. The images are data this repository redistributes
alongside the code, not a component of it.

`data/probe.npz` is a separate question with its own provenance — see
`training/README.md`.
